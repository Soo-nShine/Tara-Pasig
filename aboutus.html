About: "Tara! Pasig!"

Tara! Pasig! is a website project that encourages people to explore both locals and tourists alike to visit the city of Pasig. By providing the necessary information about the city's popular destinations, it aims to promote tourism within the city.

It presents local destinations, historical places, and recreational spots that visitors can include in their own city adventures.

Warning!

We are not affiliated with any government agencies, all information related to these sites are publicly available in the internet. If you are interested in official events, click here.

Our Purpose

Our goal is to encourage local tourism by showing people that they can discover interesting places without traveling far from home.

We also want to make exploring more engaging through quests, experience points, and achievements.

Our Mission

To encourage people to appreciate Pasig City's culture, history, and local destinations through an accessible and interactive tourism website.

How It Works

  1. Explore destinations around Pasig City.
  2. Choose a quest related to your interests.
  3. Visit places and learn about the local community.
  4. Take your time, explore, take some snaps and mark your quest as complete!
